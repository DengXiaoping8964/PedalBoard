01 — THE EXTRA TOOLS

Three ways to go further.

Each one is a normal effect in the Add effect menu. You do not need a different build, a plugin scan, or a second app.

COMPOSED · 8 KNOBS

Custom Chain: four stages in one slot.

Drive, chorus, delay and plate reverb are already wired in series. Eight knobs control the whole patch, and no stage can be left half-connected. It is the fastest way to get a complete sound without filling the board.

KnobDefaultWhat it changes
Drive30%Overdrive amount into the chain.
Tone55%Brightness of the drive stage.
Chorus25%Modulation depth after the drive.
Delay Time350 msDistance between repeats.
Feedback30%How long the repeats last.
Reverb25%Plate reverb amount.
Decay50%Reverb tail length.
Mix55%Wet/dry for the whole Custom Chain.
  • One slot, one type: you cannot add two Custom Chains to the same board.
  • Mix is the whole patch. If the sound is too wet, lower Mix before changing four other knobs.
  • All eight values are saved automatically with the project settings.
HOST · VST3

Load a plugin you already own.

Plugin Host scans nothing in the background. You point it at one .vst3 file; it exposes the first eight automatable parameters as macros and can open the plugin's own editor.

CABINET · IR

Three speakers, or your own IR.

Cabinet is convolution, not a tone knob pretending to be a speaker. It ships with 1x12 V30, 2x12 Open and 4x12 High Gain, and accepts WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG and MP3 impulse responses.

  • Built-in buttons: 1x12 V30, 2x12 Open, 4x12 High Gain.
  • Load IR… opens a file browser. The card names the loaded response.
  • Bass, Treble and Mix stay available after any IR choice.

02 — ROUTING

One chain, two paths.

The monitor path is kept fast. The virtual path adds CABLE's own buffer on top. The status bar shows the latencies separately instead of hiding them in one number.

INPUT Guitar or interface, stereo in.
CHAIN 16 slots, reordered on release, dry path compensated.
MONITOR Interface or headphones. Low latency.
VIRTUAL VB-CABLE, then a recording app.
The virtual path is not free.

CABLE adds its own buffer. Recording through CABLE Output adds that buffer on top of the monitor path. For playing, monitor through the interface; for recording, accept the extra latency or use the interface's direct monitoring.

03 — MEASURED

What can bechecked.

The repository includes an offline bench and separates what passed from what was not tested. These are implementation facts, not listening claims.

Boundaries matter more than numbers.

No listening tests are claimed; no pedal is presented as a clone of a specific hardware unit. The README lists known limits, including no VST2, no ASIO in the prebuilt release, and plugin compatibility that depends on the plugin.

04 — LIMITS

What it is not.

Knowing the edge of the tool is part of using it well. These are the limits that matter in practice.

No preset browser yet.

The current chain and every knob are saved automatically, but you cannot store and recall named presets. For now, a recipe plus a screenshot of the knobs is the workaround.

One of each effect, sixteen total.

You cannot stack two delays or two reverbs. Custom Chain exists partly to pack several stages into one slot.

No ASIO in the prebuilt release.

WASAPI, MME and DirectSound are available. ASIO requires the Steinberg ASIO SDK and a local build. Most players are fine on WASAPI.

VST3 only.

No VST2, no AU, no CLAP. A plugin that only ships in those formats cannot be hosted.

Standalone, not a DAW.

No timeline, no MIDI arrangement, no automation lanes. It is a pedalboard and a router. Recording is handled by another app such as Audacity.

No cloud presets or sync.

PedalBoardData is local. Copy it manually if you want a backup. A custom IR is referenced by path, so keep the IR file in place.
