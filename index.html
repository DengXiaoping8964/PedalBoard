01 — Why it is easy

Less setup.More playing.

PedalBoard is built for your first ten minutes, not your tenth month. The essentials are already in place, and every extra control is optional.

08effects

Gate → Compressor → EQ → Overdrive → Delay → Reverb → Cabinet → Looper. It opens with all eight on, so the first note already has a sound.

04 — First six

You do not need all 24 today.

Start with the effects that are easy to hear and hard to make sound bad. Each one below is also in the default chain or one menu away.

DYNAMICS

Noise Gate

Shuts the hiss between phrases without cutting the pick.

Tip: set Threshold just above the noise.
DYNAMICS

Compressor

Brings loud and soft picking closer together, so nothing disappears.

Tip: Ratio 4:1 is a safe first stop.
FILTER

3-Band EQ

Tames boom or harshness before it reaches the drive.

Tip: move 2–3 dB, not 10.
DRIVE

Overdrive

A little grit while the guitar still sounds like your guitar.

Tip: start around Drive 35%.
TIME

Delay

Adds repeats behind the note instead of on top of it.

Tip: keep Mix under 25% at first.
TIME

Reverb

Puts the guitar in a room and glues the repeats together.

Tip: Mix 15–25% is usually enough.
