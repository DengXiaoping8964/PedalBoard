24 effect types

Dynamics

Noise Gate

A gate with hold, hysteresis and a detector-only sidechain. It opens for the pick and closes on silence.

Start: Threshold −46 dB, Hold 10 ms, Hysteresis 3 dB.
Dynamics

Compressor

Feed-forward RMS compression with a soft knee, makeup gain and a sidechain high-pass. It makes picking feel even.

Start: Threshold −24 dB, Ratio 4:1, Makeup 6 dB.
Filter

3-Band EQ

Low shelf, mid bell and high shelf. Fix boom or harshness before it reaches the drive.

Start: all bands 0 dB; 90 / 900 / 4500 Hz. Move 2–3 dB.
Filter

Auto Wah

An envelope-driven band-pass that follows your picking. Up or Down direction; no LFO.

Start: Sensitivity 60, Min 320 Hz, Max 3600 Hz, Q 3.
Filter

Wah Pedal

Sweep by knob or MIDI expression; band-pass or low-pass. The Expression knob can drive it hands-free.

Start: Position 50%, Min 350 Hz, Max 3200 Hz, Q 3.5.
Drive

Overdrive

ADAA tanh clipping with a tube-like bias, 4× oversampled. The gentle one.

Start: Drive 35%, Tone 55%, Level 45%, Bass 0 dB.
Drive

Distortion

Hard-clip ADAA with a presence shelf and a Tighten high-pass. More aggressive than overdrive.

Start: Drive 50%, Tighten 130 Hz, Tone 45%, Level 55%.
Drive

Fuzz

Asymmetric clipping, bias and a pre-gain bass shelf. Loose, woolly, classic.

Start: Fuzz 72%, Bias 45%, Tone 58%, Level 55%.
Drive

Bitcrusher

Independent bit depth and sample-hold, with dither on every held step. Lo-fi, not broken.

Start: Bits 7, Downsample 6×, Tone 100%, Mix 100%.
Modulation

Chorus

Stereo sine chorus with Catmull-Rom interpolation and a wide spread. Thickens without detuning.

Start: Rate 0.45 Hz, Depth 38%, Delay 12 ms, Mix 50%.
Modulation

Flanger

Crossed feedback, a quadrature stereo LFO and a 1–10 ms sweep. The jet-plane sound.

Start: Rate 0.4 Hz, Depth 85%, Centre 1.5 ms, Mix 50%.
Modulation

Phaser

4, 6, 8 or 12 all-pass stages with centre and stereo spread. Softer than a flanger.

Start: Rate 0.4 Hz, Depth 70%, Feedback 40%, Mix 70%.
Modulation

Tremolo

Sine, triangle or square amplitude modulation with stereo phase spread. Volume moves, pitch does not.

Start: Rate 5 Hz, Depth 90%, Shape Sine, Spread 30%.
Modulation

Vibrato

Time-modulated delay: pitch moves, level stays. Use it for a tape-like wobble.

Start: Rate 4.5 Hz, Depth 24%, Centre 4 ms, Mix 100%.
Pitch

Octave

Sub-octave divider plus an Octavia-style upper octave. Monophonic by design — play single notes.

Start: Down 60%, Up 30%, Blend 80%.
Pitch

Pitch Shifter

Signalsmith Stretch spectral engine with independent formant control. Better for whole chords and clean shifts.

Start: Semitones +12, Formant 0, Mix 100%.
Time

Delay

1 ms–2 s, true ping-pong, tone control and optional tape wow/flutter. Modulation is off by default.

Start: Time 380 ms, Feedback 35%, Tone 60%, Mix 30%.
Time

Reverb

Dattorro plate network with pre-delay, damping and width. A room, a plate, or a long tail.

Start: Decay 55%, Damping 40%, Width 80%, Mix 30%.
Time

Cabinet

Convolution with three built-in IRs and custom WAV/AIFF/FLAC/OGG/MP3 loading. The last stage before the looper.

Start: 1x12 V30, Bass 0 dB, Treble 0 dB, Mix 100%.
Special

Ring Modulator

Input × internal sine from 20 to 5000 Hz. Metallic, robotic, deliberately strange.

Start: Frequency 440 Hz, Level 100%, Mix 60%.
Special

Looper

30 seconds of tape, overdub and one-level undo. Buttons: Rec, Play/Stop, Overdub, Clear, Undo.

Start: Level 0 dB, Tone 18 kHz, Mix 100%.
Special

Acoustic to Electric

Body, quack and air cuts plus even harmonics. A colour, not a conversion — it makes an acoustic pickup sit like an electric.

Start: Body 12 dB, Quack 8 dB, Air 6500 Hz, Drive 45%.
Composed

Custom Chain

Four curated stages — drive, chorus, delay, plate reverb — under eight knobs. One slot, one complete sound.

Start: Drive 30%, Chorus 25%, Delay 350 ms, Reverb 25%, Mix 55%.
Host

Plugin Host

Load a VST3, map its first eight automatable parameters to eight macros, and open its native editor. Path and state survive a restart.

Start: Load VST3… → choose plugin → Open Editor for the full panel.
22 pedals are built in; Custom Chain and Plugin Host are the other two types. Each type can appear once. The chain holds 16 slots. Recommendations are starting points, not rules.
